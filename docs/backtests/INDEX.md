The strong rare-event cells (ndx40/sp500/r1k) **can't be rolled yet** — ndx40 (`_mix`) fails the inference faithfulness self-check; sp500/r1k need universe refresh. They stay unproven, not disproven
- [_007: faithful_mix_inference](_007_faithful_mix_inference.md) — **fixed `_mix` inference** (root cause: a single backfilled `AZN 2026-02-09` bar broke path-dependent features; fix = align panel to the model's training row-set). **ndx40_mix now rolls — and it BEATS NDX**: +121.7% full-OOS, **57% of 50d windows beat NDX, median excess +6.6%** (vs the cell-5 family's no-edge). - [_008: roll_sp500_russell1000](_008_roll_sp500_russell1000.md) — refreshed sp500+r1k, rolled the 3 strong cells: **all beat their index** — sp500_20 **100%** of windows (med +9.5%), sp500_50 (AUC 0.90) **92%** (+7.3%), russell1000_50 **90%** over a **~3yr/105-window** OOS (+14.2%, worst −7.1%). Clean pattern: **AUC tracks the rolling edge** (high-AUC rare-event cells win; low-AUC cell-5 family doesn't). Caveat: small N (9–22 trades), overlapping windows
- [_009: nifty500_rolling](_009_nifty500_rolling.md) — **the US AUC→edge pattern does NOT transfer to NSE.** Rolled 5 nifty500 cells over a flat-to-**down** NIFTY500 tape (idx −4.8%): the AUC-0.89/0.83 +50% cells **blew up** (−9%/−56%, DD −37%/−60%); only the moderate-AUC +20%/25d showed a mild edge (+1.6% median, 57%). `_009` hypothesised regime as the cause — **but see `_010`, which refutes it** (30–65 entries, 75–80 windows, larger sample than US)
- [_010: regime_conditioning](_010_regime_conditioning.md) — **REFUTES `_009`'s regime hypothesis.** Conditioned all 667 rolling windows on the index's own return: the edge is **not** long-beta (US excess is regime-*neutral*, ρ≈0 p=0.40; both markets do *better* in down windows). Conditioning on regime does NOT close the US–NSE gap — US-down **+10.0% (98% win)** vs NSE-down **+0.2% (53%)**. So the US edge is more robust than `_008` feared (not a bull artifact) and NSE is weak in *both* regimes. Redirects to **NSE signal forensics** (is the label-AUC real/capturable?)
