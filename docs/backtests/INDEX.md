- [_010: regime_conditioning](_010_regime_conditioning.md) — **REFUTES `_009`'s regime hypothesis.** Conditioned all 667 rolling windows on the index's own return: the edge is **not** long-beta (US excess is regime-*neutral*, ρ≈0 p=0.40; both markets do *better* in down windows). Conditioning on regime does NOT close the US–NSE gap — US-down **+10.0% (98% win)** vs NSE-down **+0.2% (53%)**. So the US edge is more robust than `_008` feared (not a bull artifact) and NSE is weak in *both* regimes. Redirects to **NSE signal forensics** (is the label-AUC real/capturable?)
- [_011: nse_signal_forensics](_011_nse_signal_forensics.md) — **it's the base rate, not the AUC.** Recomputed realized labels on the fresh OOS: NSE AUC is **real** (fresh ≈ test, holds OOS) and the moves are **capturable** (entry gaps smaller than US) — *both* naive hypotheses refuted. The real gap is **absolute top-K precision**: NSE +50% top-1 wins 6–10% vs US 43–48%, because NSE events are 5–20× rarer (base 0.003 vs 0.07). **R-Precision@1 (absolute × lift>1), not AUC, predicts tradeable edge** — and it explains every `_006`–`_009` outcome. Next: wider-K / re-rank lattice by R-p@1
- [_012: rerank_wider_k](_012_rerank_wider_k.md) — **match K to precision, don't widen blindly.** Re-rank by test R-p@1 has a confounder (cell-5 tops it but has no edge → need *fresh* R-p@1 + payoff). Wider-K sweep (K∈{3,10,20}): wider-K is a **risk-reducer, not an edge-creator** — rescued the worst NSE blow-up to ~breakeven (−56%→−1%, DD −60%→−22% at K=10) but **hurt every cell that already worked** (US cells & nifty+20% best at K=3). **Optimal K tracks R-p@1 inversely**; dilution doubles as a fragility probe (nifty+20%'s +34% evaporates → was concentration-luck; US edges survive). Fixed a K-unaware dust-floor bug (0 entries at K=20); +2 tests
- [_013: precision_weighted_sizing](_013_precision_weighted_sizing.md) — **raw-p weighting can't concentrate.** New `sizing_mode="prob_weight"` (bet ∝ calibrated p). The "auto-K" hypothesis FAILS: p on rare-event cells is too flat (≈ base rate) to peak, so prob_weight spreads across the **whole universe** (r1k: 890 entries) → strongest **de-risker** yet (DDs −2% to −11%) but **destroys the high-R-p@1 edge** (sp500 92%→38%, r1k 90%→**4%** windows beat). Reinforces `_012`: concentration (hard K≈3), not p-weighting, monetizes high precision. Follow-up: sharper weights (p^α / 1/rank). +2 tests
- [_014: sharper_precision_weights](_014_sharper_precision_weights.md) — **the edge is in SELECTION, not weighting** (closes the precision-weighting thread). `prob_weight_alpha` (weight ∝ p^α), swept α∈{2,4,8} at K=20: sharpening **does not recover the edge** (sp500 stays 8–15%, r1k pinned at **4%**) and **never concentrates** — entries stay at full-universe (486/890) for *every* α, because flat p means even p⁸ can't prune the tail past the dust floor. Down-weighting ranks 4–20 ≠ excluding them. **No weighting scheme beats equal-weight top-3.** Champion unbeaten; next worthwhile work is costs/regime/bootstrap, not sizing.
