# V1.10 nifty500 canonical scan — follow-ups (parking lot)

Discovered during `docs/gbdt/V1.10_nifty500_canonical_scan_plan.md` (task #55), out of scope
for that branch. Promote to a real plan when a coherent slice is big enough.

1. **Second independent test-window replication of the +50%/200d ffund win.** The finetune
   adopted `d10/ss0.7/cs1.0` on test 2024-07→2025-06 and it replicated on the 2025-07→2026-06
   backtest — but that's still one forward window. The whole F17/F18 arc is single-window fund
   wins that failed a second window (_264, _279/_280, _285). Re-run the matched A/B on an
   independent date-aligned window before any adoption claim hardens.

2. **Finetune the broader every-K fund cells.** Phase 1 found 7 cells beating technical at
   every K; only 2 were finetuned. The common-base-rate standouts (**10%/25d** +0.124 @1,
   **20%/100d** +0.120 @1, **10%/100d** +0.084 @1) are the natural next finetunes — likely
   stronger deploy candidates than the rare 50%/200d (whose 200d room caveat truncates
   backtest entries).

3. **NIFTY index benchmark in run_fresh_oos.** The harness hardcodes `^NDX` (US) as the
   benchmark line — meaningless for nifty500. Add `^NSEI` (NIFTY 50) as the reference for NSE
   cells (needs the index seeded into nse_equities first; not currently cached).

4. **nifty500 /daily-predictions deploy path.** No forward-serving cadence exists for NSE cells
   today (infer_fresh + the forward log are us_equities-only). If a nifty champion is ever
   promoted, the serving path (NSE seed + roster pin + regime gate on a NIFTY index) is net-new.

5. **screener.in backfill provenance hardening.** The pre-2019 panel uses screener annual
   revenue/net_income (validated ~0% median discrepancy vs XBRL) but holds shares constant at
   the earliest split-adjusted basis and carries a 2019 screener→XBRL seam (~39% NaN at the
   handoff). A cleaner quarterly pre-2019 source (or seam smoothing) would tighten it.
