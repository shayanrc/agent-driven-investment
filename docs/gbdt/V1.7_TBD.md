## Gating step before any fundamentals promotion

1. **Second independent date-aligned window (THE gate).** `_272` showed F18
   fundamentals improve BOTH sp500 champions on the 2024-H2 date-aligned window
   (AUC + test Brier down on both; top-K net-positive). This is materially stronger
   than the F17 macro attempt (`_264`), and notably it's already on the conservative
   window where macro's edge evaporated — but it is still **one window**. Before
   wiring fundamentals into `/daily-predictions` or swapping a champion, re-run the
   same matched-HP A/B (`base_v2` vs `fund`, single fit) on a **second** date-aligned
   test window (e.g. an earlier split fraction, or the trailing window) and confirm
   AUC + Brier + top-K replicate. Only a two-window replication justifies promotion;
   a champion swap remains a separate human decision (`_019`). This is the direct
   analogue of the `_262`→`_264` macro re-validation.

## Robustness

2. **Defensive duplicate-`fiscal_period_end` guard in `valuation.ttm.build_ttm_timeline`.**
   (Carried from `docs/valuation/V2_TBD.md` §1, relevant because F18 consumes the
   valuation panel.) The TTM consecutiveness check keys on a `>110`-day gap, so a
   *duplicate* fiscal_period_end (0-day gap) would slip through and could double-count
   a quarter. Can't happen today (us_fundamentals is PK'd by grid date), but a
   defensive drop-duplicates/assert would harden it before the panel is trusted for
   deployment.

## Feature reach (only if the gate passes)

3. **Per-arm FS/HP tuning of the fundamentals model.** `_272` is a single-fit
   matched-HP A/B (clean isolation of the +9 columns). If fundamentals replicate on a
   second window, a full agent-driven FS+HP tune of the `all_fundamentals` arm could
   push past the base champion's envelope (the A/B measured the *marginal feature*
   effect, not the *tuned* ceiling).

4. **More fundamentals features.** F18 v1 is 9 columns (yields + xs-ranks + rev-YoY +
   earnings-yield change). Candidates if the family proves out: margin trend
   (net-income/revenue), FCF-margin, P/FCF and PS *levels* (not just yields),
   accruals, sequential (QoQ) growth, and balance-sheet ratios once
   `us_fundamentals` gains balance-sheet columns (`data_pipelines` V3_TBD §2).
